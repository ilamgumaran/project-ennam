## 1. முதலில் — the definition is not in this chapter

The first surprising fact. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் opens with an arithmetic announcement and never defines
its own term. The definition of மெய்ப்பாடு sits two chapters later, in **செய்யுளியல் நூற்பா 196**,
where மெய்ப்பாடு is being enumerated as one component of a poem:

> உய்த்துணர்வு இன்றி தலைவரு பொருண்மையின்
> மெய்ப் பட முடிப்பது மெய்ப்பாடு ஆகும்.	196

*(pmuni0100 lines 3795–3796)* [மூலம் | SOURCE]

**Word by word:**

| சொல் | பிரிப்பு | பொருள் | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| உய்த்துணர்வு | உய்த்து + உணர்வு | ஊகித்து அறிதல் | inferential grasp; understanding you have to *lead yourself* to |
| இன்றி | — | இல்லாமல் | without |
| தலைவரு | தலை + வரு | முன்வந்து சேரும் | that comes to the front, arrives of itself |
| பொருண்மையின் | பொருண்மை + இன் | பொருள்தன்மையால் | by the meaning-nature |
| மெய்ப் பட | மெய் + படு | உடலில் / உண்மையில் தோன்ற | so as to become body / become manifest |
| முடிப்பது | — | முடிவு கொள்வது | that which completes, concludes |

**Reading:** *what concludes by becoming manifest — through a meaning that arrives at the front
without needing to be inferred — that is மெய்ப்பாடு.* [விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]

The load-bearing word is **உய்த்துணர்வு இன்றி — without inference.** The whole category is defined
by *not* being a decoding operation. This is the sharpest single line in the chapter's neighbourhood
and the one most relevant to `appaal/`; §12 returns to it.

The very next நூற்பா ties the definition back to this chapter:

> எண் வகை இயல் நெறி பிழையாதாகி
> முன்னுறக் கிளந்த முடிவினது அதுவே.	197

*(lines 3797–3798)* [மூலம் | SOURCE] — "not straying from the path of the eight kinds, it is of the
conclusion already stated." **The cross-reference is the text's own; the eight of மெய்ப்பாட்டியல்
are what செய்யுளியல் means by மெய்ப்பாடு.** [விளக்கம்]

### 1.1 மெய்ப்பட — a standing idiom, counted

`மெய் பெற` / `மெய்ப்பட` is not coined for this chapter. It is Tolkappiyam's working phrase for
*"so that it takes substantial form"*, and it occurs **22 times across all three அதிகாரம்**
(`grep -c "மெய் பெற\|மெய்ப் பட\|மெய்ப்பட\|மெய்பெற\|மெய் பெற்ற"` → 22). It is used of a consonant
taking its shape (செய்யுளியல் 3, line 3399: `ஒற்றொடு வருதலொடு மெய்ப் பட நாடி`), of உரிச்சொல்
senses (உரியியல் 85, line 1969: `மெய்ப்படத் தோன்றும் பொருட்டு ஆகும்மே`), and — in the final
நூற்பா of the entire work, மரபியல் 112 — of how a teacher should test a doctrine
(line 4137: `உய்த்துக்கொண்டு உணர்த்தலொடு மெய்ப்பட நாடிச்`). [மூலம் | SOURCE]

**So மெய்ப்பாடு ≠ "emotion".** It is *the becoming-embodied of something that was not yet in a
body.* Emotion is the case; embodiment is the category. [விளக்கம்]

### 1.2 The letter-level rhyme — sourced, not decorative

Tolkappiyam's first chapter, நூல் மரபு, names its two classes of letter:

> ஔகார இறுவாய்ப்
> பன்னீர் எழுத்தும் உயிர் என மொழிப.	8
> னகார இறுவாய்ப்
> பதினெண் எழுத்தும் மெய் என மொழிப.	9
> மெய்யொடு இயையினும் உயிர் இயல் திரியா.	10

*(lines 90–94)* [மூலம் | SOURCE] — twelve are called **உயிர்** (life); eighteen are called **மெய்**
(body); *and joining with the body does not alter the life's nature.*

**[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]** The same figure runs the whole grammar twice. At the letter layer,
உயிர் becomes audible only by taking a மெய். At the layer of feeling, an inner state becomes
knowable only by taking a மெய். Tolkappiyam is not using "body" metaphorically in
மெய்ப்பாடு — it is using its own established structural term. The build in `appaal/meippadam/`,
which renders exactly the உயிர்/மெய் atoms as vocal-tract glyphs, sits inside this figure without
having known it.

## 2. The chapter's opening arithmetic — and an honest problem

> பண்ணைத் தோன்றிய எண் நான்கு பொருளும்
> கண்ணிய புறனே நால் நான்கு என்ப.	1
> நால் இரண்டு ஆகும் பாலுமார் உண்டே.	2

*(lines 3208–3210)* [மூலம் | SOURCE]

**பண்ணை** is glossed inside Tolkappiyam itself. உரியியல் 21:

> கெடவரல் பண்ணை ஆயிரண்டும் விளையாட்டு.	21

*(line 1893)* [மூலம் | SOURCE] — கெடவரல் and பண்ணை both mean **விளையாட்டு, play.**
So நூற்பா 1 literally opens: *the thirty-two meanings that appeared in **play**.* The chapter's
first word places emotion's grammar in the register of enacted play — not of psychology, not of
ethics. [விளக்கம், resting on Tolkappiyam's own gloss]

**The arithmetic, laid out honestly:**

| Phrase | Plain reading | What the chapter actually enumerates |
|---|---|---|
| எண் நான்கு | 8 × 4 = **32** | ✅ நூற்பா 4–11 give exactly 4 causes for each of 8 = 32 |
| நால் நான்கு (the "புறன்", the outside) | 4 × 4 = **16** | ❓ the next list (நூற்பா 12) has **32** items, not 16 |
| நால் இரண்டு | 4 × 2 = **8** | ✅ நூற்பா 3 lists exactly 8 |

**[திறந்த கேள்வி | OPEN QUESTION]** In *this* edition the "நால் நான்கு = 16" of நூற்பா 1 does not
match any list I could count in the chapter. Commentaries divide on this நூற்பா and I have **no
local commentary on this இயல்** to consult. I record the mismatch rather than manufacture a
resolution. What can be said without a commentary: the chapter opens by announcing **three**
enumerations (32 / 16 / 8), and the eight of நூற்பா 3 are explicitly the *smallest* of them —
the eight are a division *within* a larger scheme, not the scheme itself.

## 3. நூற்பா 3 — the eight

> நகையே அழுகை இளிவரல் மருட்கை
> அச்சம் பெருமிதம் வெகுளி உவகை என்று
> அப் பால் எட்டே மெய்ப்பாடு என்ப.	3

*(lines 3211–3213)* [மூலம் | SOURCE]

| # | மெய்ப்பாடு | Literal | Gloss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **நகை** | laughter | laughing, mirth |
| 2 | **அழுகை** | weeping | crying, grief |
| 3 | **இளிவரல்** | *the coming-low* | being brought low; the state of one degraded — **not** "disgust" in the sense of the one who feels revulsion (see §7) |
| 4 | **மருட்கை** | bewilderment | astonishment, being struck |
| 5 | **அச்சம்** | fear | fear |
| 6 | **பெருமிதம்** | *the being-made-great* | exaltation, high pride |
| 7 | **வெகுளி** | wrath | anger |
| 8 | **உவகை** | gladness | joy |

**[விளக்கம்]** Six of the eight are named by a **verbal noun of what the body does or undergoes** —
நகை *laughing*, அழுகை *weeping*, இளிவரல் *coming-low*, மருட்கை *being-bewildered*, பெருமிதம்

convention itself is behavioural, not introspective. This matters for §11.

### 4.1 What the causes reveal about the design

**[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]** Read the four columns of causes as a set and the architecture shows:

- **The causes are in the world, not in the mind.** அச்சம் is caused by a deity, a beast, thieves,
  and *your own king* — four external agents, not four mental appraisals. வெகுளி is caused by
  maiming, dispossession, harassment, killing — four things done *to* you. The grammar runs
  **world → state → body**, and only the last arrow is what the poet writes.
- **அச்சம்'s fourth cause is a political fact.** தம் இறை — one's own sovereign — is listed alongside
  demons, beasts and bandits as a standard cause of fear. That is a Sangam-era social datum sitting
  inside a grammar rule.
- **பெருமிதம் is the one category with no negative cause.** Learning, courage, fame, giving: all
  four are achievements. It is the only member of the eight whose causes are all things a person
  *did*. [கருதுகோள் | HYPOTHESIS — testable by asking whether Sangam புறம் poems attach பெருமிதம்
  markers only to these four situations.]
- **புணர்வு — sexual union — is a *cause of உவகை*, not a category of its own.** This is the single
  most consequential fact in the list for §7.

| 4 | நாணுதல் · துஞ்சல் · அரற்று · கனவு |

| 7 | கையாறு · இடுக்கண் · பொச்சாப்பு · பொறாமை |

**[விளக்கம்]** This list is a different kind of thing from the eight, and the difference is the most
interesting formal fact in the chapter. It mixes, without marking the seams:

- **virtues** — நடுவுநிலை impartiality, அடக்கம் restraint, அன்பு love, அருளல் grace
- **acts of mind** — கருதல் considering, ஆராய்ச்சி investigating, நினைதல் remembering, சூழ்ச்சி scheming
- **conditions of the body** — வியர்த்தல் sweating, நடுக்கு trembling, உயிர்ப்பு breathing/sighing,
  துஞ்சல் sleeping, கனவு dreaming
- **failures** — பொச்சாப்பு heedlessness, மடிமை sloth, பொறாமை envy, ஏழைமை-adjacent states
- **speech-events** — வாழ்த்தல் blessing, அரற்று crying out

A modern taxonomy would separate emotion, virtue, cognition, physiology and speech-act into five
tables. Tolkappiyam puts them in one, because its criterion is not *what kind of inner thing is
this* but **what shows.** Sweating and impartiality belong together because both are things a
reader can be shown. That is a formal decision, and it is the chapter's real theory.
[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION — the criterion is my reading; the co-listing is source.]

*(lines 3259–3262)* [மூலம் | SOURCE] — such others too, joined with these, being settled acts, are
called **நிமித்தம் — signs.**

### 6.1 The 24-numbering is confirmed from a local file

**[மூலம் | SOURCE — cross-verified]** Naccinarkkiniyar, commenting on அகத்திணையியல் 54 in
`pmuni0500_01-tholkappiyam-porulathikaram-urai-1.txt` line 3104, writes:
*"தேறுதல் ஒழிந்த காமத்து மிகுதிறம்- **இருபத்து நான்காம் மெய்ப்பாட்டில்** நிகழ்ந்து் ஏழாம் அவதிமுதலாக
வரும் அறிவழிகுணன் உடையளாதலும்"* — "**in the twenty-fourth மெய்ப்பாடு**…". The commentator numbers
these signs **consecutively 1 to 24**, and the twenty-fourth is the last item of நூற்பா 18,
கையறவு உரைத்தல். The internal grading 1–6 × 4 is therefore also an external numbering 1–24. This
is the only direct handle on this chapter available anywhere in the local commentary corpus.

### 6.2 What the sequence is

**[கருதுகோள் | HYPOTHESIS]** Read in order, the twenty-four are a **monotonic scale of disclosure**:
grade 1 is involuntary and *concealed* (she hides the smile, shows no disorder); grade 2–3 is
displaced fidgeting with hair, ornament, dress; grade 4 is speech; grade 5 is consent; grade 6 is
collapse. Nothing in the நூற்பா says "stages of a courtship" — the text gives only the ordinals.
The progression from *hiding* to *breaking* is visible in the items themselves, and is why I read
them as graded rather than merely numbered.

**[திறந்த கேள்வி]** What the six ordinals index — successive meetings, successive stages of களவு,
or degrees of intensity — is exactly what a commentary would say and what I cannot get from the
மூலம் alone. The signs are also, on their surface, almost entirely the **தலைவி's** body. Whether
that is the text's restriction or the commentary's is open.

### 7.2 What the text says about rasa: nothing

**[மூலம் | SOURCE — negative evidence, reproducible]** `grep -n "சுவை" pmuni0100-tholkappiyam-moolam.txt`
returns **three** hits in the entire work, at lines 1277, 2043, 2813:

- line 1277 — `வண்ணம் வடிவே அளவே சுவையே` — colour, shape, measure, **taste**: சுவை as one of the
  physical குணம் of an object, in a list of case-relations.
- line 2043 — `வண்ணத்தின் வடிவின் அளவின் சுவையின் என்று` — the same four, as attribute-compounds.
- line 2813 — `ஏனது சுவைப்பினும் நீ கை தொட்டது` — *tasting* food.

**Tolkappiyam uses சுவை only for physical taste. It never uses it aesthetically, and the word does
not occur in மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் at all.** The chapter contains no term for what an audience feels.

### 7.3 What the commentators do

**[மூலம் | SOURCE]** The local commentary files show Naccinarkkiniyar (14th c.) using
**எண்வகைச் சுவை** — "the eight kinds of rasa" — as an established phrase, and mapping it onto
மெய்ப்பாடு:

- `urai-1` line 2937: *"இஃது **எண்வகைச் சுவையான் வரும் மெய்ப்பாடுங்** கூத்தோடும் படுதலின் அச்சுவை
  பற்றிவரும் மெய்ப்பாட்டிற்கும் உரித்தாயிற்று."* — "since **the மெய்ப்பாடு that arises from the
  eight kinds of rasa** also belongs with dance…"
- `urai-2` line 4217: *"கூத்தராயினார் **எண்வகைச் சுவையும் மனத்தின்கட்பட்ட குறிப்புக்களும் புறத்துப்
  போந்து புலப்பட** ஆடுவார்"* — "those who are dancers dance **so that the eight rasas and the
  intentions lodged in the mind come outward and become manifest**."
- `urai-1` line 3219, on why a poetic convention is not simply fiction: *"இல்லதென்று கேட்டோர்க்கு
  **மெய்ப்பாடு பிறந்து இன்பஞ்செய்யா** தாகலானும்"* — "because [if it were called unreal]
  **மெய்ப்பாடு would not be born in the hearers and give pleasure**."
- `urai-1` line 100, listing the eight-fold analysis of பொருள்: *"**எட்டுவகை மெய்ப்பாடும்**, நால்வகை
  உவமமும், ஐவகை மரபு மென்பர்"*.

### 7.4 The seam, laid out — not resolved

**[திறந்த கேள்வி | OPEN QUESTION]** I am not going to resolve this. Here is the honest ledger.

| | Tolkappiyam's மூலம் | The commentators, from the local files |
|---|---|---|
| Word for aesthetic flavour | none — சுவை is physical taste only | **எண்வகைச் சுவை**, used freely and without argument |
| Where the state lives | in the **agent** — the person whose four causes acted on them | **"born in the hearers"** (கேட்டோர்க்கு … பிறந்து) |
| What it produces | manifestation (மெய்ப்பட) | **இன்பம்** — pleasure |
| Domain | play/performance (பண்ணை), poem-component | **கூத்து**, dance-drama |

**The internal evidence that the eight are not the eight rasas:**

1. **புணர்வு — sexual union — is a *cause of உவகை* (நூற்பா 11), not a category.** If Tolkappiyam
   were transposing a rasa scheme, the erotic would be a member, and in the Sanskrit list it is
   conventionally the first. Here it is one of four inputs to joy, sitting beside *play* and *wealth*.
   [விளக்கம், resting on நூற்பா 11]
2. **The whole of அகம் — the love-life that occupies four of the nine chapters of பொருளதிகாரம் —
   is not one of the eight.** A rasa scheme keyed to Tamil poetics that omitted love as a category
   would be a strange transposition.
3. **வியப்பு — wonder — is listed in நூற்பா 26 among things that must be *absent*.** [மூலம்]
4. **The chapter's own criterion is causal and behavioural** (world → state → body), not receptive.
   Nowhere does a நூற்பா of this chapter mention a hearer, a reader, or an audience — until 27.
5. **இளிவரல் is not "disgust".** It is the *coming-low* — and its four causes (நூற்பா 6) are age,
   disease, affliction, feebleness. Those are causes of **being pitiable**, not of *feeling revolted*.
   The most-cited Sanskrit correspondence for slot 3 does not survive contact with the four causes.
   [விளக்கம் — this one I hold with some confidence, because the causes are explicit]

**The evidence on the other side:** the list is eight; a commentator working eleven-plus centuries
later reads it as eight rasas without feeling the need to argue; and நூற்பா 1's பண்ணை (play) plus
நூற்பா 27's eye-and-ear (§8) genuinely do put the chapter in a performance frame.

**[வெளி அறிவு — EXTERNAL, NOT FROM ANY LOCAL SOURCE FILE]** For completeness: the eight rasas of
the Sanskrit dramaturgical tradition are conventionally śṛṅgāra, hāsya, karuṇa, raudra, vīra,
bhayānaka, bībhatsa, adbhuta. **I have no local file for the Nāṭyaśāstra and have not verified this
list or any of its dating against a source in this repo.** It is here only so the shape of the
comparison is visible; it must not be cited from this document as though it were sourced.

**[திறந்த கேள்வி — dating]** Which text is earlier is contested and I will not state it. Positions
on Tolkappiyam's date range across many centuries and different layers of the work are dated
differently by different scholars; positions on the Nāṭyaśāstra likewise. `varalaru/arivu-varalaru.md`
holds this repo's ledger on dating disputes. Anyone who tells you the direction of influence is
settled is telling you their position, not the evidence.

## 8. நூற்பா 27 — the chapter's last word, and its best

> கண்ணினும் செவியினும் திண்ணிதின் உணரும்
> உணர்வுடை மாந்தர்க்கு அல்லது தெரியின்
> நல் நயப் பொருள்கோள் எண்ண அருங்குரைத்தே.	27

*(lines 3294–3296)* [மூலம் | SOURCE]

**Word by word:**

| சொல் | பொருள் | English |
|---|---|---|
| கண்ணினும் செவியினும் | கண்ணாலும் காதாலும் | by the eye and by the ear |
| திண்ணிதின் உணரும் | உறுதியாக அறியும் | who grasp firmly |
| உணர்வுடை மாந்தர்க்கு அல்லது | உணர்வுள்ள மனிதர்க்கு அல்லாமல் | except to people who have உணர்வு (feeling / the faculty of being moved) |
| தெரியின் | ஆராய்ந்தால் | if examined |
| நல் நயப் பொருள்கோள் | நல்ல நயத்துடன் பொருள் கொள்ளுதல் | the taking of meaning with fine relish |
| எண்ண அருங்குரைத்தே | எண்ணுதற்கு அரியது | is hard even to conceive |

**Reading:** *Except for people of உணர்வு, who grasp firmly through the eye and the ear — the
taking-of-meaning-with-relish is, on examination, scarcely even thinkable.* [விளக்கம்]

**[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]** After twenty-six நூற்பா of taxonomy, the chapter closes by naming
its own precondition — and the precondition is **a receiver who is already tuned.** The channels are
named: **eye and ear.** The requirement is named: **உணர்வு** — not education, not knowledge of the
codebook, but the faculty of being moved. And what fails without it is not comprehension but
**நயம்** — relish. You can decode without உணர்வு; you cannot *taste*.

This is the one நூற்பா in the chapter that turns to face the audience, and it is the last one.
The architecture is deliberate: the taxonomy is stated as a grammar of the *performer's* body, and
only at the end is it revealed that the whole apparatus is worthless against an untuned receiver.

## 9. அகம் / புறம் — where the chapter sits

**The chapter itself does not partition its material into அகம் and புறம்.** That is a finding, not
an omission on my part: the words அகம் and புறம் as திணை-categories do not organise any நூற்பா of
this இயல். [மூலம் — by inspection of lines 3207–3296]

What can be said from the source:

- **The eight are neutral to the divide.** வெகுளி's causes (maiming, seizure of one's people,
  harassment, killing) are புறம் situations — war and violation. உவகை's causes (wealth, senses,
  union, play) run across both. பெருமிதம்'s (learning, courage, fame, giving) are the standing
  virtues of **புறம்** panegyric. அச்சம்'s fourth cause is *one's own king*. **The eight are a
  layer beneath the திணை system, not a subdivision of it.** [விளக்கம்]
- **The twenty-four signs (13–18) are unmistakably அகம்.** Hair, earring, dress, the hidden meeting,
  consent, collapse — this is களவு material, and its placement here rather than in களவியல் is
  itself the argument that the *display* of feeling is a separate technical subject from the
  *conduct* of love. [விளக்கம்]
- **நூற்பா 22–26 are அகம் conduct**: love-sickness (கலக்கம்), the eight marks of undiminishing
  union, the ten excellences, the ten matchings, the twelve absences.
- **The chapter's real placement is between பொருளியல் and உவமயியல்** — that is, between the chapter
  on how poetic matter behaves and the chapter on simile. It is filed with **craft**, not with
  **subject matter.** [மூலம் — chapter order, lines 3062 / 3207 / 3298]

### 9.1 The rest of the grammar cites it back

மெய்ப்பாடு is load-bearing outside its own chapter — three citations, all verbatim:

1. **உவமயியல் 19** (lines 3345–3346):
   > பெருமையும் சிறுமையும் மெய்ப்பாடு எட்டன்
   > வழி மருங்கு அறியத் தோன்றும் என்ப.	19

   — greatness and smallness in a simile appear as known **by way of the eight மெய்ப்பாடு**. The
   eight are the yardstick for whether a comparison lifts or lowers. [மூலம்]
2. **செய்யுளியல் 1** (line 3386): `பயனே மெய்ப்பாடு எச்ச வகை எனாஅ` — மெய்ப்பாடு is one of the
   **thirty-four உறுப்பு of a செய்யுள்**, listed between பயன் (result) and எச்சம் (residue).
   The count 34 is the நூற்பா's own: `ஆறு தலை இட்ட அந் நால் ஐந்தும்` (4×5 + 6 = 26) plus the eight
   of `அம்மை அழகு தொன்மை தோலே / விருந்தே இயைபே புலனே இழைபு`. [மூலம் | SOURCE]
3. **செய்யுளியல் 196–197** — the definition and its back-reference (§1).

**[விளக்கம்]** Point 2 is the structural claim: **in Tolkappiyam's anatomy of a poem, emotional
manifestation is a component on the same list as syllable, foot, line, metre, and rhyme.** Not a
result of the poem. A *part* of it — as countable and as specifiable as அசை or தொடை.

## 10. காட்டுதல், சொல்லாமை — showing rather than stating

The task asked what the text says about showing rather than stating feeling. The strongest material
is outside மெய்ப்பாட்டியல், and it points in two directions at once.

**For showing:**

- **உள்ளுறை உவமம்** — அகத்திணையியல் 51 (lines 2293–2294):
  > உள்ளுறுத்து இதனொடு ஒத்துப் பொருள் முடிக என
  > உள்ளுறுத்து இறுவதை உள்ளுறை உவமம்.	51

  — that which, being *placed inside*, concludes with "let the meaning complete itself as matching
  this" — that is உள்ளுறை உவமம். [மூலம்]
- **இறைச்சி** — பொருளியல் 33–34 (lines 3154–3156): `இறைச்சிதானே உரிப் புறத்ததுவே.` — the இறைச்சி
  stands *outside* the உரிப்பொருள்; and `இறைச்சியின் பிறக்கும் பொருளுமார் உளவே / திறத்து இயல்
  மருங்கின் தெரியுமோர்க்கே` — there are meanings *born from* the இறைச்சி, for those who discern.
  [மூலம்]
- **குறிப்பு மொழி** — செய்யுளியல் 172 (lines 3734–3735):
  > எழுத்தொடும் சொல்லொடும் புணராதாகி
  > பொருட்புறத்ததுவே குறிப்பு மொழியே.	172

  — *not joined with letter or with word, standing outside the meaning: that is குறிப்பு மொழி.*
  [மூலம் | SOURCE]

  **[விளக்கம்]** This is the cleanest statement of implicature in the grammar. It defines a class of
  speech by what it is **not attached to** — not to the letters, not to the words, not even to the
  stated meaning. Tolkappiyam has a formal category for *what the poem does not say*.

**Against a naive "show, don't tell":**

- **பொருளியல் 51** (lines 3197–3203) — the counterweight, and it is severe:

  > ஒப்பும் உருவும் வெறுப்பும் என்றா
  > கற்பும் ஏரும் எழிலும் என்றா
  > சாயலும் நாணும் மடனும் என்றா
  > நோயும் வேட்கையும் நுகர்வும் என்று ஆங்கு
  > ஆவயின் வரூஉம் கிளவி எல்லாம்
  > நாட்டு இயல் மரபின் நெஞ்சு கொளின் அல்லது
  > காட்டலாகாப் பொருள என்ப.	51

  [மூலம் | SOURCE] — likeness, form, revulsion; chastity, beauty, grace; softness, shame, modesty;
  sickness, desire, enjoyment — **all these are meanings that cannot be shown (காட்டலாகாப் பொருள)
  unless the heart takes them in the manner of the country's own custom.**

  **[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]** Tolkappiyam explicitly says that inner-state words are
  *undemonstrable*. They cannot be pointed at. They arrive only when the நெஞ்சு takes them
  **நாட்டு இயல் மரபின்** — by the settled custom of the land. This is a statement that the transfer
  of interiority is **convention-dependent all the way down.** Held next to செய்யுளியல் 196's
  *உய்த்துணர்வு இன்றி*, the grammar is saying both: the manifestation arrives without inference,
  *and* it arrives only inside a shared custom. Those are not contradictory — inference is a
  conscious operation, custom is a prior tuning — but the pair is the whole design problem of
  `appaal/`, stated in the seventh century or earlier. See §12.

- **மரபியல் 110** (lines 4105–4114) lists the சிதைவு, the defects, and two of them are receptive:
  `கேட்போர்க்கு இன்னா யாப்பிற்று ஆதல்` — being of a construction painful to hearers — and
  `என்ன வகையினும் மனம் கோள் இன்மை` — in whatever manner, **not being taken by the mind.** [மூலம்]
  Failure to land is a formal defect of the composition, not a failure of the audience.

## 11. What this chapter is doing, and what it is not

The task asked for the deepest thing. Here it is, as precisely as I can put it.

### 11.1 What it is

**A grammar of emotion is a taxonomy of *legible surfaces*, written for producers.** Every design
decision follows from that:

- **The units are named for what the body does.** நகை, அழுகை, இளிவரல், மருட்கை, பெருமிதம், உவகை —
  six of eight are verbal nouns of visible behaviour or visible condition (§3). The category is not
  "the feeling of X" but "the X-ing".
- **The causes are external and enumerable.** Four each, closed lists, in the world: a beast, a
  king, a loss, an act of giving (§4). This makes the system **compositional**: given a situation,
  the correct manifestation is derivable. That is what a *grammar* is for. A phenomenology would
  need none of this.
- **Physiology, virtue, cognition and speech-act are co-listed** (§5), because the sorting criterion
  is showability, not ontology.
- **The signs are graded and ordinally indexed** (§6): 1 through 6, four apiece, running from
  concealment to collapse. This is a **scale**, and scales are for choosing — a poet at stage 3
  must not write a stage-6 sign.
- **The output is a component of a poem** (§9.1): item 20 of 34, next to metre and rhyme.
- **The whole system is declared void without a tuned receiver** (§8), in its last நூற்பா.

**[கருதுகோள் | HYPOTHESIS]** The chapter is best read as a **specification for encoding interiority
into observable behaviour, given a shared cultural codebook** — a spec written from the encoder's
side, with the decoder's requirement stated once, at the end, as a precondition rather than a
procedure.

### 11.2 What it is not

- **It is not psychology.** There is no claim about what an emotion *is*, no interior mechanism, no
  faculty, no seat, no account of how a cause produces a state. The arrow from world to feeling is
  asserted and never examined.
- **It is not ethics.** நூற்பா 24–26's excellences and absences are ethical, but they are attached
  to **conduct in love**, not to the eight. Nothing says wrath is wrong. Wrath has four causes and
  a set of visible signs, and that is the entire treatment.
- **It is not, on its own text, a rasa theory** (§7). It has no word for aesthetic taste, no
  audience-side vocabulary until நூற்பா 27, and its eight are keyed to causes in the agent.
- **It is not a theory of expression as release.** Nothing here concerns catharsis, sincerity, or
  the value of feeling felt. The signs are *legibility engineering*.
- **It does not claim universality.** பொருளியல் 51 says the opposite in as many words: these
  meanings are takeable only **நாட்டு இயல் மரபின்**, by the custom of the land (§10).

### 11.3 Why this is unusual

**[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION — this is a comparative claim and I hold it loosely]** Grammatical
traditions ordinarily stop at the sentence. Tolkappiyam's பொருளதிகாரம் keeps going — past syntax,
past semantics, into what a poem is *about* and how a person *shows*. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் is the point
where a work that began by counting the thirty letters (`எழுத்து எனப்படுப / அகரம் முதல் / னகர இறுவாய்
முப்பஃது என்ப`, lines 68–70) arrives at enumerating the four causes of weeping. **The same
book, the same method, the same closed-list technique, applied to the letter அ and to grief.**

That continuity is the deepest fact here. It is not that Tamil happened to also have a treatise on
emotion. It is that **emotion was treated as one more layer of the language's own structure**, with
the identical formal apparatus — enumerate, close the list, state the count, give the causes, cite
the cross-reference. The உயிர்/மெய் figure of the first chapter (§1.2) is the figure of this one.
A grammar that classes eighteen letters as *bodies* animated by twelve *lives* is a grammar already
committed to the proposition that inner things become knowable by taking outer form. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல்
is that proposition run once more, at the top of the stack.

**[திறந்த கேள்வி]** Whether any other grammatical tradition does this is a comparative-literature
question I cannot answer from local sources. I am not going to assert "no tradition anywhere else"
from priors.

## 12. For `appaal/meippadam/` — what the chapter gives it, and what it takes away

The build at `appaal/meippadam/` proposes **articulatory space as the substrate two differently-built
minds can share**, and puts one claim at risk in `aaivu/sothanai-007-meeturuvakkam.md`: that Ilam can
reconstruct an encoded felt-state from a glyph **cold, without the codebook** — "meaning transferred
with near-zero learning."

### 12.1 What the chapter gives it

1. **The name is better than it knew.** மெய்ப்படம் was coined as *body-picture*. But **மெய்ப்பட** is
   Tolkappiyam's own idiom for *becoming-manifest*, used 22 times across the work (§1.1), and
   **மெய்ப்பாடு is defined as `உய்த்துணர்வு இன்றி … மெய்ப் பட முடிப்பது`** — *what completes by
   becoming manifest, without inference* (§1). The build's central claim — transfer without a
   decoding step — is the classical definition of the word it is named after. That is not a
   coincidence to celebrate; it is a **precedent to be held to**. [விளக்கம்]
2. **உயிர்/மெய் is not a naming convention, it is the thesis** (§1.2). The build renders உயிர் and
   மெய் atoms as vocal-tract glyphs. The grammar's own first move is to call twelve letters *life*
   and eighteen *body*, and to say the life is unchanged by taking a body. The build is standing
   inside a figure the source works out at three levels.
3. **A worked precedent for a closed generative code over felt states.** `appaal/README.md` §2.8
   wants states mapped and transitions engineered. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் has **states** (8), **causes**
   (32, four per state, closed), and **a graded transition scale** (24 signs, ordinally indexed
   1→6, running concealment→collapse). That last is the nearest thing in the corpus to the
   மாற்றம் engine the build says is unbuilt. It is small, it is one-directional, and it is confined
   to one dramatic situation — but it exists, it is enumerated, and it is 24 items, not a metaphor.
   [முன்மொழிவு | PROPOSAL — the 24 are worth reading as a candidate transition ladder for build 02.]
4. **`meippadam.py`'s `state_to_atom(energy, brightness)` has a classical counterpart.**
   The chapter's move is exactly the same shape: a felt state is decomposed into enumerated,
   externally-specifiable dimensions, and rendered into a channel the receiver already reads.
   Tolkappiyam picks the dimensions from the *world* (four causes); the build picks them from the
   *body* (aperture, frontness). [விளக்கம்]

### 12.2 What the chapter takes away

This is the part the build needs.

1. **பொருளியல் 51 is a direct counterexample to near-zero-learning transfer.** [மூலம் | SOURCE]
   The grammar states in its own words that நோய், வேட்கை, நாண், மடம், வெறுப்பு — precisely the
   felt-states meippadam wants to encode — are **காட்டலாகாப் பொருள**, meanings that cannot be shown,
   *except* as the heart takes them **நாட்டு இயல் மரபின்**, by the custom of the land. The oldest
   sustained study of showing-feeling in this language concluded that showing feeling is
   **convention-bound**. `appaal/README.md` §3.1 already names the tension — the "coded gap" whose
   codebook is priced in tuition — but the build's test claims to escape it. **The source says the
   escape is not available at the level of the felt-state word.** This should be written into
   `sothanai-007` as a stated prior, not discovered after the test. [கருதுகோள் — the prediction
   this yields: reconstruction should succeed for gross dimensions (energy, aperture) and fail for
   *named* states, because it is the naming that is convention-bound.]
2. **நூற்பா 27 makes the receiver a precondition, not a variable.**
   `கண்ணினும் செவியினும் திண்ணிதின் உணரும் / உணர்வுடை மாந்தர்க்கு அல்லது` — only for people who have
   **உணர்வு**. `appaal/README.md` §2.5 already carries Ilam's condition — "anyone who **can**
   resonate" — as part of the channel. **The chapter says the same thing and puts it last**, after
   the entire apparatus, which is a structural comment: build the whole spec, then admit it is
   inert without a tuned receiver. And note what §3.5 (2026-08-01) demands: divergence must stay
   visible. நூற்பா 27 gives no procedure for detecting an untuned receiver — it only names the
   requirement. **That gap is still open, 1,500+ years later, and the build has not closed it
   either.** [திறந்த கேள்வி]
3. **The channels are named, and one of them the build does not have.**
   `கண்ணினும் செவியினும்` — **eye and ear.** `appaal/meippadam/README.md` lists "no sound yet" as a
   v0 limit. The source treats the two as a pair in its single statement about reception. The
   commentary is stronger still: `urai-2` line 4217 has dancers making the inner குறிப்பு
   *"புறத்துப் போந்து புலப்பட"* — come outward and become manifest — in **கூத்து**, which is motion
   in time. **Two of the chapter's three framings (பண்ணை = play, கூத்து = dance) are temporal
   media, and meippadam v0 is static.** The 24 graded signs are all *actions*: loosening hair,
   removing one earring, raising both hands. **Not one of the 24 is a static configuration.**
   [மூலம் — lines 3240–3258]
4. **What the chapter does *not* offer the build, plainly.**
   - **No articulatory content whatsoever.** The chapter is about face, brow, hair, hands, dress,
     sweat, trembling, sleep — it never touches the vocal tract. The build's substrate hypothesis
     gets **no support** from its namesake. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல்'s body is the *visible* body; பிறப்பியல்,
     in எழுத்ததிகாரம், is the *articulating* body. They are different chapters with different
     subjects, and the build inherits the name of the first while implementing the second.
     [விளக்கம் — this is worth stating in `meippadam/README.md`.]
   - **No two-mind problem.** The chapter assumes one culture, one codebook, one shared மரபு.
     `appaal/README.md` §3.3's open hole — *two forks share physics; two minds share what?* — is
     not addressed, because for Tolkappiyam the answer was never in doubt: they share நாட்டு இயல்
     மரபு. The build is asking a question the source did not have.
   - **No account of transfer.** The chapter specifies what to show. It never says how showing
     becomes knowing in another head. நூற்பா 27 asserts that it does, for the tuned, and stops.

### 12.3 One proposal

**[முன்மொழிவு | PROPOSAL]** `sothanai-007` currently tests reconstruction of *felt states* from
glyphs. மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் suggests a sharper split that would make a null result informative rather
than merely negative: test **graded dimension** and **named state** separately. The 24 signs are
graded (1→6) *and* named; if Ilam can recover the **grade** of an encoded state without the codebook
but not its **name**, that is a real result — it would locate the convention-boundary exactly where
பொருளியல் 51 puts it, and it would mean the shared tuning is real but thinner than claimed.
If he recovers neither, the articulatory-substrate hypothesis is in trouble. If he recovers both,
பொருளியல் 51 has a counterexample and that is worth `aaivu/karuthu/`.
