# இலக்கணம் | The Grammar Shelf — read from the source

தொல்காப்பியம் and the grammatical tradition, read **from the மூலம்** rather than from summaries.
Every நூற்பா on this shelf is quoted exactly from a citable edition and numbered as printed; the
local copies are in `../_src/txt/` with a manifest at `../_src/README.md`.

Where `../paadam/` teaches the grammar as lessons and `../marabu/` finds it inside poems, this shelf
holds **what the text actually says**, so both can rest on something checkable.

## நிலை | Standing caution

**Commentary is mostly unread — but not entirely, and the difference matters per document.**
`tholkappiyam-thinai.md` **does** cite நச்சினார்க்கினியர் from `pmuni0500_01`, which is local.
The other four read the bare நூற்பா only; சேனாவரையர் on சொல்லதிகாரம் and இளம்பூரணர் on
செய்யுளியல் are not in `../_src/` at all. Reading a sutra without its உரை is a real
methodological position and a real limitation — `../varalaru/arivu-varalaru.md` §5
warns that the commentary can be the bias *and* the key, and the warning runs both ways.

Documents here marked as drafted under the corpus sweep of 2026-08-23 were produced by subagent
readers working from the local sources, and **every Tamil quotation in them was grep-verified against
the source file** before the document entered the repo. Verification catches invention; it does not
catch misreading. The ஐயம் sections are where each document says what it is unsure of.
