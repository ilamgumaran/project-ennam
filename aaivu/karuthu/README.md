# கருத்துப் பாதை | Idea Evolution Records

This folder preserves how a research claim changes under pressure. A journal says **what happened**; an experiment says **what was tested**; an idea-evolution record says **why the current position is no longer the starting position**.

Use one when a question passes through disagreement, a counterexample, new evidence, or a material revision. Preserve the losing and surviving arguments. Do not polish the path into inevitability.

## உறவுகள் | What goes where

- `../ellai.md` — the expressive or conceptual limit that opened the question
- `../vithai.md` — a capacity or pattern that may answer it
- this folder — the claim's evolution and evidence ledger
- `../sothanai-*` — a hypothesis put at risk by a declared protocol
- `../../naatkurippu/` — the chronological session record
- `../../isaivu.md` — first-person observations about the collaboration

## வடிவம் | Reusable structure

```markdown
# [number] · [Tamil title] | [English title]

**Date:**
**Status:** starting claim / active hypothesis / tested / revised / concluded
**Sources:**

## கேள்வி | Question

## தொடக்கக் கூற்று | Starting claim

## எதிர்வாதம் | Challenge or counterexample

## ஆதாரப் பதிவு | Evidence ledger

| Item | State | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | source claim / observation / hypothesis / prediction / experiment result / conclusion | ... | ... |

## திருத்தம் | Revision

## இப்போதைய நிலை | Current position

## சோதிக்கக்கூடிய முன்னறிவுகள் | Testable predictions

## திறந்த ஐயங்கள் | Open uncertainties

## செலுத்துவது | Feeds
```

The status words follow `../../murai.md` §9. A source may contain its own words such as “validated” or “proven”; the record still classifies the imported statement by the evidence visible to this project.
