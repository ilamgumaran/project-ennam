# கருத்துப் பாதை 002 · எதுகை — how a claim about Tamil rhyme moved

**Opened and closed 2026-08-23.** Recorded per `../../murai.md` §10: preserve the full arc, do not
rewrite the starting point to make the final position look inevitable.

## கேள்வி | The question

Is எதுகை — agreement on the **second** letter — a governing feature of classical Tamil verse, and
was it always?

## தொடக்கக் கூற்று | The starting claim

Stated in [`../../marabu/paadal/003`](../../marabu/paadal/003-kurunthogai-002-kongu-ther.md) §7 on
2026-08-22, from the pattern of exactly two texts, and labelled a hypothesis:

> "எதுகை is a post-Sangam systematisation — present but not governing in ஆசிரியப்பா, near-universal
> by the Kural."

It was reached honestly and it was under-evidenced: குறள் 1 has a clean எதுகை (அ**க**ர / ப**க**வன்),
குறுந்தொகை 2 has none between its அடி, and the inference was drawn from that contrast alone. §11 of
that entry named தொல்காப்பியம் **செய்யுளியல்** as the thing that would settle it, and said so before
the answer was known.

## சவால் | The challenge

செய்யுளியல் was read from `_src/txt/pmuni0100`, நூற்பா 84–96 quoted in full
([the reading](../../ilakkanam/tholkappiyam-seyyuliyal.md)). The text refuses half the claim
outright.

## சான்று | The evidence

**Against Part A — "post-Sangam".**

> மோனை எதுகை முரணே இயைபு என
> நால் நெறி மரபின தொடை வகை என்ப. (84)
> அஃது ஒழித்து ஒன்றின் **எதுகை** ஆகும். (89)

எதுகை is **named** (84), **defined** (89), **licensed for kindred letters** (90) and
**sub-classified by interval** (94, 95) inside Tolkāppiyam itself. On any date in the contested
range it is the grammar of the Sangam corpus or its immediate neighbourhood. **எதுகை did not arrive
after the Sangam. It was systematised early.**

**For Part B — "not governing".** Four textual mechanisms, of which one is decisive:

> சொல்லிய தொடையொடு வேறுபட்டு இயலின்
> சொல் இயற் புலவர் அது **செந்தொடை** என்ப. (96)

நூற்பா 96 gives a name, with authority, to a line that **differs from every தொடை just listed**. A
verse with no tie is not defective; it is a *செந்தொடை* verse. **A prosodic system that names the
absence of rhyme as its own legitimate category is one in which rhyme is not obligatory.**

Supporting: (b) நூ. 86's inventory is permissive to the point of casualness — *அமைத்தனர் தெரியின்
அவையுமார் உளவே*, "if you look into what they composed, those exist too"; (c) **தளை** is assigned to
particular பா compulsorily and repeatedly, while **no நூற்பா anywhere assigns a தொடை to a பா** —
தளை is constitutive, தொடை is ornamental; (d) எதுகை is defined *negatively off* மோனை — *அஃது ஒழித்து
ஒன்றின்*, "in the one other than that" — and has no independent definition.

**And a measurement.** Part B was then put at risk against eight corpora with a declared protocol, a
shuffled-partner chance baseline, and a pre-stated failure condition (*if ஆசிரியப்பா and வெண்பா show
the same ratio, Part B is dead*). எதுகை between consecutive அடி, first சீர் to first சீர்:

| corpus | பா | rate | ÷ chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| திருக்குறள் | குறள்வெண்பா | 56.8 % | **9.7 ×** |
| கலித்தொகை | கலிப்பா | 48.4 % | **8.4 ×** |
| நாலடியார் | வெண்பா | 49.5 % | **7.8 ×** |
| ஐங்குறுநூறு | ஆசிரியப்பா | 29.6 % | 4.8 × |
| குறுந்தொகை | ஆசிரியப்பா | 24.4 % | 4.2 × |
| நற்றிணை | ஆசிரியப்பா | 17.0 % | 2.7 × |
| பதிற்றுப்பத்து | ஆசிரியப்பா | 14.0 % | 2.6 × |

The failure condition did not trigger. Rates are **lower bounds** — the printed word is not the
சீர், and some e-text lines are merged, both of which can only push rates down.

## திருத்தம் | The revision

- **"Post-Sangam" is struck.** It was wrong and the word comes out of entry 003.
- **"Not governing in ஆசிரியப்பா" stands**, and is now better supported than when it was guessed —
  14–30 %, one line-junction in four to seven. A constraint obeyed a quarter of the time is not a
  constraint. And it is *real* rather than absent: 2.6–4.8× chance, never at chance.
- **"Near-universal by the Kural" is not confirmed** at 56.8 %. The miss-inspection explains why and
  is itself a finding: in **குறள் 21** the எதுகை is *inside* line 1 — ஒ**ழு**க்கத்து /
  வி**ழு**ப்பத்து — with line 2 not participating. **Valluvar anchors எதுகை on the opening சீர் but
  is free about where its partner sits.** Entry 001 softened accordingly.
- **Both entries' மோனை readings are corroborated,** which was not the point of the exercise and is
  the more valuable result. Across அடி, மோனை sits at chance in **all seven** corpora (0.9–1.0×);
  within an அடி it is above chance in **all seven** (1.5–2.4×). Tamil poets alliterated foot-to-foot
  inside a line, not line-to-line. Entry 001's `அகர … ஆதி` and entry 003's `காமம் … கண்டது` are both
  within-line readings, and both are now backed by ~23,000 lines of verse and by நூ. 88 and 94–95.

## தற்போதைய ஐயம் | Current uncertainty

- **[கருதுகோள்]** The historical change is not that எதுகை *appeared* but that it **migrated** — from
  an optional within-line tie between சீர் to a compulsory between-line tie anchored on the first
  சீர் of each அடி. Untested.
- **[கருதுகோள்]** கலித்தொகை behaves like வெண்பா (8.4×), and நூ. 104 already groups them —
  *வெண்பா நடைத்தே கலி*. A two-thousand-year-old classification predicting a measured result deserves
  a proper `sothanai-*`.
- **[எல்லை]** No commentary was consulted. இளம்பூரணர் and நச்சினார்க்கினியர் on செய்யுளியல் are not
  in `_src/`. Every reading here is of the bare நூற்பா.
- **[எல்லை]** The measurement counts **printed words**, not சீர். Until a real foot-segmenter exists,
  every rate above is an approximation whose *comparisons* are sound and whose *absolute values* are
  not.
- **காதுக்காக:** the numbers say ஆசிரியப்பா has எதுகை at 2.6–4.8× chance without being governed by
  it. Does that match the ear — does Sangam akaval *sound* rhymed to you, occasionally rhymed, or
  not rhymed at all?
