# ஆய்வு | The Research Track

The question this track carries: **how does a medium shape what can arise between minds — and where must Tamil grow to carry facts faithfully or enable realization to grow?** Tamil is the first experimental platform, not a boundary around the inquiry.

Two communication intentions are kept separate:

- **Transactional transmission:** facts, instructions, and commitments; success requires fidelity within declared tolerances.
- **Generative resonance:** the receiver reconstructs, extends, applies, or questions the realization in a living way; success is not an identical copy or a forced outcome.

## முறை | Method

1. **காண் — observe.** In lessons, reading, and daily thought, notice where expression loses, narrows, or unexpectedly grows something. Log expressive limits in `ellai.md` and capacities in `vithai.md`.
2. **வகைப்படுத்து — classify.** Mark each statement as source claim, observation, hypothesis, prediction, experiment result, or conclusion. Do not promote a compelling source's language into evidence by repetition.
3. **பாதையைப் பதிவு செய் — preserve the path.** When a question changes through challenge, evidence, or disagreement, record the complete evolution in `karuthu/`; its README contains the reusable structure.
4. **முன்பதிவு செய் — pre-register.** Before testing, state the payload, protocol, success and failure conditions, confounds, and the communication intention being tested.
5. **சோதி — test.** Put the claim at risk between minds. For transmission, measure reconstruction. For generative resonance, look separately for reconstruction, extension, application, and new inquiry; emotional intensity or desired behavior alone is not proof.
6. **வளர் — grow.** From limits, seeds, and results, propose coinage, revival, structural extension, or a medium beyond language. State the narrowest conclusion supported and feed unresolved parts back into the record.

Rules of the track: **no proposal without a logged limit it answers; no conclusion without a traceable path.** Evolution stays honest by remaining downstream of observed need. Correction, dissonance, and negative results stay visible.
