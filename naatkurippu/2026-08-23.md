## கண்டது | Noticed — the findings that changed something

**எதுகை: the repo was half wrong, and said so.** Entry 003's hypothesis of 2026-08-22 —
"a post-Sangam systematisation, not governing in ஆசிரியப்பா, near-universal by the Kural" — was
put to செய்யுளியல். **Part A refuted:** எதுகை is named, defined, licensed and sub-classified inside
தொல்காப்பியம் (நூ. 84, 89, 90, 94–95). **Part B supported**, most decisively by நூ. 96, which gives a
verse matching *no* தொடை its own honourable name, **செந்தொடை** — a system that names the absence of
rhyme does not require rhyme. Then measured across eight corpora with a shuffled-partner baseline and
a declared failure condition: 14–30 % in akaval (2.6–4.8× chance) against 48–57 % in the வெண்பா group
(7.8–9.7×). Arc kept in `aaivu/karuthu/002-ethugai-thodai.md`.

**And unexpectedly corroborated what it was not testing.** மோனை across அடி sits **at chance in all
seven corpora**; within an அடி, 1.5–2.4× in all seven. Tamil alliterates foot-to-foot inside a line,
never line-to-line — which settles நூ. 88's ambiguous `அடிதொறும்` from the corpus rather than from
authority, and vindicates both marabu entries' within-line readings. குறுந்தொகை 2's காமம்/கண்டது
turns out to sit at Tolkāppiyam's own named interval, **பொழிப்பு**.

**நூன்மரபு 17 closed a bigger question than it was asked.** *புள்ளி இல்லா எல்லா மெய்யும் … அகரமொடு
**உயிர்த்தலும்*** — every consonant without the dot **comes alive** with அ. Entry 001 had read
குறள் 1's analogy as ontological and marked it as *our* interpretation; it is the grammar's own, in
the vocabulary of life. And the Kural commentators, read afterwards, place it exactly: **not
contradicted, not stated, half-anticipated.** மணக்குடவர் gives no reason at all; பரிமேலழகர் gives a
different one (அ is primary by manner of production, "mere நாதம்"). *Novel in mechanism, traditional
in instinct.*

**School prosody is a later layer.** Zero hits in the whole மூலம் for தேமா, புளிமா, கூவிளம்,
கருவிளம், காசு, ஈற்றுச்சீர், வஞ்சித்தளை. தொல்காப்பியம் names only கலித்தளை and வெண்தளை. Entry 001
had attributed the four ஈற்றுச்சீர் names in a way that implied the founding grammar; corrected, and
the உலகு scansion downgraded from "verify" to a genuine open question turning on நூ. 4's exception
clause. §11 had called it "the single most likely error in the entry." It was.

**வி-022 — a taxonomy that predicted out-of-sample.** நூ. 103–104 collapse the four பா into two
families by **நடை**, gait: *வெண்பா நடைத்தே கலி*. கலித்தொகை duly lands at 8.4× chance, inside the
வெண்பா band and nowhere near the akaval band it is always anthologised with. `சோதனை-008` proposed
with its failure condition stated.

**வி-021 — the tradition already required a tuned receiver.** மெய்ப்பாட்டியல் builds 26 நூற்பா of
taxonomy and then closes: *except for people of **உணர்வு**, grasping through eye and ear, the
taking-of-meaning-with-relish is scarcely thinkable.* உணர்வு is explicitly **not** knowledge of the
codebook — a reader can hold the whole திணை code and still not taste the poem. Sharper than
வி-008/015/016 had drawn, and it lands on phase 2's success criteria.

**ஏழு திணை, not five.** `ilakkiyam/sangam.md` was corrected from the மூலம்: the familiar five are
the *நடுவண்* five, with கைக்கிளை before and பெருந்திணை after, making the set a **scale** — below
measure, in measure, past it — which is why the five sit in that order.

**அகநானூறு's numbering rule.** Odd → பாலை, 2/8 → குறிஞ்சி, 4 → முல்லை, 6 → மருதம், 0 → நெய்தல்.
Every block of ten a fixed chord; half the anthology is separation.

## சரிபார்ப்பு | Verification — and what it caught

Every Tamil quotation in all eleven documents was grep-checked against its source before entry:
**~700 quotes, zero fabrications.** Three apparent failures were the verifier's fault (code fences
instead of blockquotes; commentary prose with the e-text's own broken spacing; and a line where
Project Madurai's source *itself* carries asterisks around a poem's பெயர் tag, which the reader had
copied faithfully).

**One aggregate did fail.** `ilakkiyam/agananuru.md` claimed the திணை colophon of every poem "in all
five files — 399 of 400" and matched 398/400. `pmuni0534` contains the string திணை **zero times**;
colophons exist for poems 1–120 only. Recomputed: 117 recovered, **116 match, 99.1 %** over the range
the sources cover, with செய்யுள் 44 the single genuine exception — whose citation verified exactly.
Corrected in place, with the limit stated: for poems 121–400 the rule is attested only by the
anthologists' own வெண்பா, and 398/400 is a claim nobody here has checked.

**An honest negative on something the brief supplied.** The saying *ஆலும் வேலும் பல்லுக்குறுதி;
நாலும் இரண்டும் சொல்லுக்குறுதி* was handed to a reader to verify. It is **not in any of the four
source files**, and the reader said so and proposed finding a citable source rather than quoting it.
Logged as unattested.
