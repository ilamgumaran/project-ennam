## கண்டது | Noticed

**உரியியல் defines a word-class by its opacity** — வி-023.

> **வெளிப்படு சொல்லே கிளத்தல் வேண்டா / வெளிப்பட வாரா உரிச்சொல் மேன.** (உரியியல் 2)

*A word whose meaning comes out plainly needs no explaining; **உரிச்சொல் are those that do not.***
A formal word-class constituted by **not explaining itself** — and having named it, the chapter
spends itself glossing its members one sutra at a time. A grammar carrying a dictionary of its own
difficulty. வி-008 found reader-completion in *forms*, வி-021 in *persons*; this is the same
requirement at the level of the **word**, and the tradition gave it entry conditions and a gloss
convention. The phase-2 hypothesis that follows: **a coinage that fails as a compound might succeed
as a உரிச்சொல்.**

**The எண்/எழுத்து ladder closed.** Entries 002 and 005 had been climbing something without knowing
where it went. Entry 008 §7 found the top: the same claim on all three rungs, always at the letter
**எ** — ஆத்திசூடி's *don't despise them* → கொன்றைவேந்தன்'s *they deserve to be called eyes* →
குறள் 392's *they **are** the eyes of the living*. Prohibition, comparison, definition. The child's
book is not simpler material; it is the Kural's claim cut to a size a child can hold. This repo is
named **எண்ணம்**, from **எண்** — and குறள் 393 is why we should never quote 392 without it.

**புறம் 192 is not the poem the slogan makes it.** Its colophon reads **துறை: பொருண்மொழிக் காஞ்சி**,
and காஞ்சி is the புறத்திணை of **impermanence**. The tradition filed *யாதும் ஊரே* not as universal
brotherhood but as a **meditation on transience**. The edition even titles it **பெரியோர் சிறியோர்**
— by its ending, not its famous opening — and the ending is *ranked*: not awed by the great, and
scorning the small **அதனினும்**, even less than that.

## பிழைகள் | Two of my own, both found by checking

**The stripper had been silently destroying a source.** The original HTML strip deleted `<br>` and
`<p>` **without inserting a newline**. Harmless where the source HTML carried its own line breaks;
catastrophic where it did not. **புறநானூறு** — 648 KB — became a **40-line** file whose longest line
was **607,835 characters**, and *that* is the file a subagent was handed on 2026-08-23 and told to
read closely. All 43 files re-stripped; புறநானூறு went from 40 lines to **7,036**.
`ilakkiyam/purananuru.md` was re-verified against the repaired file and stands (44 of 46), but that
was the reader coping, not the tooling working. The check that would have caught it on day one is
one line of awk, and is now recorded in `../_src/README.md`.

**I truncated `marabu/README.md` to zero bytes.** A Python edit that opened the file for writing and
then read it inside the same expression — `open(p,'w').write(open(p).read().replace(...))` — empties
the file before the read runs. Recovered immediately with `git checkout --`, which is the entire
argument for committing often. Redone by reading once up front and writing once at the end.
