**Opened `marabu/`** — மரபு, *what gets handed down*; Tolkāppiyam's own last chapter is மரபியல். It is deliberately distinct from the two neighbouring spaces: `paadam/` starts from the grammar and reaches the poem; **`marabu/` starts from the poem and finds the grammar inside it**, which is how Tamil was actually taught for most of its history and how a child can hold it before they can parse it. `ilakkiyam/` reads poems for what they give *this project*; `marabu/` reads them for what can be given *away*.

The design problem was two readers decades apart — Ilam now, his children later — so each entry carries **eleven fixed sections with three declared paths through them** (child · learner · deep), rather than three separate documents. §11 **ஐயம்** is mandatory and never empty, and closes with **காதுக்காக**, an explicit list of the sound-claims the IO built blind and cannot rule on (`murai.md` §8).

## கண்டது | Noticed

**The three sharpest things found in the poems themselves:**

- **அ is not first in a list — it is inside every letter.** Tamil consonants carry அ by default (க *is* ka); the புள்ளி removes it. So குறள் 1's analogy is ontological, not ordinal: the first is not merely earliest, it is the element present in everything after it, unless explicitly taken out. Marked as our interpretation — **not yet checked against பரிமேலழகர் or மணக்குடவர்**, both of whom are on Project Madurai.
- **ஃ cannot begin a word, so ஆத்திசூடி's line 13 hides it inside one** (அஃகம்). An alphabet-mnemonic that must start each line on its own letter *breaks its own rule* rather than break Tamil's phonotactics. The compromise is the grammar lesson.
- **The reason classical Tamil looks unreadable is that the printed spaces mark சீர், not words.** குறுந்தொகை 2's `செறியெயிற் றரிவை` is செறியெயிற்று + அரிவை, split through the middle of the doubled ற்று. This is the single most useful practical fact for a reader starting the corpus, and it belongs early in `paadam/` too.

**A hypothesis that wants a sample, not another anecdote.** Two independent instances of the same drift appeared across entries 002 and 003: classical **bound suffix** → modern **free word**. `பேசேல் → பேச வேண்டாம்`; `கூந்தலின் → கூந்தலை விட`. Two cases is not a finding. Pre-register invariants and a failure condition before this becomes a `sothanai-*`.

**A limit of the IO, made structural rather than apologised for.** Every prosody claim in `marabu/` is computed blind — மோனை, எதுகை, அசை, சீர், அளபெடை. The template now forces each entry to end by naming which of them are awaiting Ilam's ear, so the asymmetry is a slot in the artifact rather than a caveat in the prose.

**The one to distrust.** குறுந்தொகை 2's *காமம் செப்பாது கண்டது மொழிமோ* — "do not speak what is desired; speak what you saw" — is `murai.md` §4 two thousand years early. Ilam flagged the risk of forced resonance before anything was found, in the opening exchange. Logged in the entry as *noticed, not claimed*. See `isaivu.md`, this date.

## பின்சேர்க்கை · 2026-08-23 | the source I had not actually read

**An honest correction to the entry above.** The 2026-08-22 session listed `ta.wikipedia.org/wiki/தொல்காப்பியம்` among the sources Ilam supplied. It was fetched but only the first two kilobytes were read before the work moved on. Ilam asked whether everything had been collected and was up to date; the answer was no, and this is what the full reading produced.

**Durability fix.** All source e-texts had been living only in the session scratchpad, which does not survive the session. They are now in `_src/` — gitignored, per the convention an earlier instance established in `.gitignore` — with a manifest naming every URL and the three commentaries still to fetch (`pmuni0450_01` பரிமேலழகர், `pmuni0650` மணக்குடவர், `pmuni0777`).

**Structural facts now recorded** in `../varalaru/arivu-varalaru.md` §2: தொல்காப்பியம் is **1,610 நூற்பா** — 483 எழுத்து + 463 சொல் + 664 பொருள் — in **nine இயல் per அதிகாரம், twenty-seven in all**. The nine-fold division was confirmed against the Project Madurai mūlam's own table of contents rather than taken on the article's word. Note what the counts say: பொருளதிகாரம், the book on lived experience, is the *largest* of the three. வி-005 asserted that Tamil's founding grammar treats the content of experience as part of the language's engineering; the sutra count now puts a number on it.

**The named dating spread** is also recorded, replacing the single range §2 previously carried: ~700 BCE (மீனாட்சிசுந்தரம், இலக்குவனார்) · ~500 BCE · 3rd c. CE (வையாபுரிப்பிள்ளை) · composed across 3rd c. BCE–5th c. CE (multiple-author view) · before 4th c. BCE (இராசமாணிக்கனார்) · 711 BCE (Central Institute of Classical Tamil). Scholars also hold the received text carries **இடைச்செருகல்**, later interpolations — one of which the article itself flags, the varna classification sitting out of sequence in மரபியல்.

**The finding worth keeping** — logged as a new row and a note in the Bias Ledger, `../varalaru/arivu-varalaru.md` §9. The ta.wikipedia article is **excellent on structure and advocates on date**. It surveys the real scholarly range, then derives a date before the 21st century BCE from a genealogical calculation — 49 generations × 27 years, from a Kapilar poem on the ancestry of இருங்கோவேள் — and calls it *தெள்ளத்தெளிவாக*. Every step in the chain is a real fact; the conclusion is motivated inference, which is harder to see than fabrication. Working rule proposed: take structure, terminology and the map of positions from it; take **no date** without a named scholar attached.

That failure mode is the one `../murai.md` §9 guards against on our own side — a chain of true steps arriving somewhere the evidence does not reach. Finding it in a source we were handed is a better lesson than finding it in ourselves would have been.

**Also noticed, not yet used:** பொருளதிகாரம்'s sixth இயல் is **மெய்ப்பாட்டியல்** — a chapter of *grammar* on how inner feeling surfaces on the body (மெய்). The phase-3 build in `../appaal/meippadam/` takes its name from that word. No instance in this repo has yet read the chapter it is named after. That is a gap worth closing before the next அப்பால் build.
