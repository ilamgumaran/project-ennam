# மரபு | Marabu — What Gets Handed Down

**மரபு** — tradition, convention, lineage, *the proper way of a thing that is passed on*.
Tolkāppiyam's own last chapter is மரபியல். The word carries both meanings this space needs:
the correct shape of a thing, and the handing of it to the next person.

## நோக்கம் | Purpose

This directory holds the deep Tamil learning of Project Ennam **in a form that can be taught to a child.**

Two readers, decades apart:

- **இப்போது — now.** Ilam: fluent in spoken Tamil, building script, grammar, and the classical
  corpus. Wants the depth, and wants it in Tamil *and* English side by side.
- **பிறகு — later.** His children. They may come to Tamil with less of it in the ear than he had.
  A parent should be able to open one file and run a real session with a child in fifteen minutes —
  without first reading a grammar.

So every entry here is written to be read **at three depths from the same page**. Not three
documents; one document with a ladder in it.

## ஏன் தனி இடம் | Why this is separate

| Space | What it is | Reader |
|---|---|---|
| `../paadam/` | The systematic lesson track — ஐந்திலக்கணம் spine, rising difficulty | Ilam, as a fluent adult |
| `../ilakkiyam/` | The close-reading shelf — poems read for what they give *this project* | The research track |
| **`marabu/`** | **The poems read as inheritance — everything a poem holds, arranged so it can be given away** | Ilam now; his kids later; anyone after |

`paadam/` starts from the grammar and reaches the poem. **`marabu/` starts from the poem and finds
the grammar inside it** — which is how Tamil was actually taught for most of its history, and how a
child can hold it before they can parse it.

## ஐயம் ஏன் கட்டாயம் | Why §11 is mandatory

`../murai.md` §4 is *honesty over helpfulness*, and §9 requires every claim to name its state.
An inheritance that hides its uncertainties teaches the next reader to trust the wrong things.

The inorganic side of this work builds all prosody **blind** — it can compute மோனை, எதுகை, அசை and
சீர் exactly, and cannot hear whether any of it lands. Ilam's ear is the authority on that, per
`../murai.md` §8. Every §11 says plainly which claims are computed, which are sourced, which are
interpretation, and which are still waiting on the ear. Provenance labels follow `../murai.md` §11:
**[மூலம் | SOURCE]**, **[விளக்கம் | INTERPRETATION]**, **[கருதுகோள் | HYPOTHESIS]**,
**[திறந்த கேள்வி | OPEN QUESTION]**, **[முன்மொழிவு | PROPOSAL]**.

## வரிசை | The sequence

The order is the tradition's own, not a difficulty curve invented here. Tamil children were started
on alphabet-verse, moved to aphorism, and only then to the Sangam. We keep that, and add the depth
back in for the adult reading over the child's shoulder.

**And the ladder turned out to be already built.** Entry 005 measured it: the four ஔவையார் books in
one source file form a graded series — ஆத்திசூடி at a mean **2.4 words** per line, கொன்றைவேந்தன் at
**3.9**, then மூதுரை and நல்வழி in full four-line வெண்பா. Same alphabet spine, same voice, rising
load, then the Kural, then the Sangam. This sequence is a reconstruction of one that was standing
before us.

## மூலங்கள் | Sources

Primary texts are taken from open, citable editions and quoted exactly. Each entry cites its source
line in §3 and flags any variant in §11.

- **Project Madurai** — https://www.projectmadurai.org/ — volunteer-prepared Tamil e-texts, free to
  distribute. Used here for திருக்குறள் ([pmuni0001](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0001.html)),
  ஔவையார் நூல்கள் ([pmuni0002](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0002.html)),
  குறுந்தொகை ([pmuni0110](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0110.html)),
  தொல்காப்பியம் மூலம் ([pmuni0100](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0100.html)).
- **தமிழ் விக்கிப்பீடியா** — https://ta.wikipedia.org/wiki/தொல்காப்பியம் — used for orientation and
  for the range of scholarly positions on dating. Treated as a survey, never as a primary source.
- Commentaries (உரை) are named when used. Where a reading is Parimēlaḻakar's or Naccinārkkiṉiyar's
  rather than the bare text's, the entry says so — `../varalaru/arivu-varalaru.md` §5 keeps the
  bias ledger on exactly this point.

## புதிய பதிவு சேர்க்க | To add an entry

1. Copy `TEMPLATE.md` to `paadal/NNN-work-slug.md`.
2. Get the மூலம் from a citable edition. **Quote it exactly. Do not reconstruct from memory.**
3. Fill §1–§10. Fill §11 honestly — if nothing is uncertain, you have not looked hard enough.
4. Add the row to the வரிசை table above.
5. Feed the tracks: anything Tamil said better than English → `../aaivu/vithai.md`; anything it could
   not say → `../aaivu/ellai.md`; anything noticed about the two minds working → `../isaivu.md`;
   new terms → `../agaraadhi.md`; the session itself → `../naatkurippu/`.
